The Arabic path

Every student follows the same path at their own pace. The placement test tells you where you start.

  1. ١

    Stage 1

    The letters

    ا ب ت ثا ب ت ثا ب ت ث

    Alif, bā’, tā’, thā’: the first letters

    Recognise, pronounce and write all 28 letters, working on where each one is articulated.

    Al-Qaida An-Nuraniya, Nur Al-Bayan

  2. ٢

    Stage 2

    Reading

    كَتَبَكتبكَتَبَ

    Kataba: “he wrote”

    Read words, then sentences, with their vowels and without hesitating, and write from dictation.

    Nur Al-Bayan, guided reading

  3. ٣

    Stage 3

    The language

    بَيْتٌ كَبِيرٌبيت كبيربَيْتٌ كَبِيرٌ

    Baytun kabīrun: “a big house”

    Understand and speak: everyday vocabulary, first grammar rules, short conversations.

    Madinah books (volumes 1 and 2), Al-Arabiya Bayna Yadayk

  4. ٤

    Stage 4

    Mastery

    الْعِلْمُ نُورٌالعلم نورالْعِلْمُ نُورٌ

    Al-‘ilmu nūrun: “knowledge is light”

    Read authentic texts, go deeper into grammar and express yourself precisely.

    Madinah books (volume 3), Al-Furqan
